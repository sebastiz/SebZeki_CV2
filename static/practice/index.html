About My Practice

I am a consultant gastroenterologist based at St Thomas' Hospital, London, specialising in upper gastrointestinal conditions. I see patients with a wide range of digestive problems and offer both diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy.

My research background in oesophageal physiology, Barrett's oesophagus, and gastrointestinal cancers directly informs the care I provide, ensuring patients benefit from the most current evidence and techniques.
